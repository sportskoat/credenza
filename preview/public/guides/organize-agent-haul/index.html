Organize an agent haul without a spreadsheet

If your haul lives in tabs, Notes, and a sheet of raw URLs, you need a shelf—not another marketplace.

Who this is for

People who already use shopping agents

You buy through Superbuy, Sugargoo, Kakobuy, CNFans, Hoobuy, or similar. Finds land from Reddit, Yupoo albums, Weidian, and Taobao. The pain is chaos before the order—not discovery of a new catalog.

Job

One place for the decision

Keep every candidate on a shelf with photo + buy link, size context, and status. When you are ready, open the agent. Credenza does not replace checkout.

Steps

How Credenza organizes the haul

  • Stash — paste a link or multi-URL line.
  • Card — cover, title, price, seller on one surface.
  • Size — body profile + chart recommendation when you have a chart.
  • Status — Want before payment, Bought after payment.
  • Buy — open preferred agent from the canonical URL.
The two statuses

Want before payment. Bought after payment.

Credenza records one buying decision. Use Want while you consider an item. Use Bought after you pay. Keep later warehouse photos on the card.

  • Want — a candidate. Nothing is paid.
  • Bought — paid. The card keeps the purchase and its photos together.

Track QC photos covers the warehouse-photo workflow in detail.

Hauls

A haul is a parcel, not a folder

You group items into a haul because they ship together. A haul carries a name, a budget, and the parcel weight and dimensions. The shelf then adds up what is inside it, so you see the parcel total before you pay for shipping, not after.

Prices arrive in the currency the store used. The shelf converts each one to USD for the total, so a haul that mixes CNY and USD still adds up to one number.

Photos

The album is the reason a link is worth keeping

A Yupoo album is how you decide. A raw URL in a sheet is not. Credenza pulls a cover photo and up to six photos onto the card, and keeps the album link so you can open the rest externally. Six is a deliberate cap: it is enough to judge the item, and it keeps the shelf fast on a phone.

QC photos from the agent are stored on the same card as the find, so the picture you bought from and the picture you received sit together.

Agents

Switching agents does not break the shelf

Credenza stores the marketplace URL, never the agent URL. The agent link is built when you press Buy. Change your preferred agent in settings and every item on the shelf opens at the new one. Nothing stored changes.

This is why a retired agent is not a dead shelf. When an agent stops serving your country, you pick another one and your links still work. See choose an agent and open a Weidian link in your agent.

Tradeoff

When a spreadsheet is still fine

If you buy one item a month and never compare size charts, a sheet works. If you plan multi-item parcels, re-check QC, or re-open old Yupoo albums, a planner pays for itself in fewer wrong sizes and lost links.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
