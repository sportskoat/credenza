Who this is for

People who already use shopping agents

You buy through Superbuy, Sugargoo, Kakobuy, CSSBuy, or similar. Finds land from Reddit, Yupoo albums, Weidian, and Taobao. The pain is chaos before the order—not discovery of a new catalog.

Job

One place for the decision

Keep every candidate on a shelf with photo + buy link, size context, and status. When you are ready, open the agent. Credenza does not replace checkout.

Steps

How Credenza organizes the haul

  • Stash — paste a link or multi-URL line.
  • Card — cover, title, price, seller on one surface.
  • Size — body profile + chart recommendation when you have a chart.
  • Pipeline — Want → Bought → QC → GL/RL → Shipped.
  • Buy — open preferred agent from the canonical URL.
Tradeoff

When a spreadsheet is still fine

If you buy one item a month and never compare size charts, a sheet works. If you plan multi-item parcels, re-check QC, or re-open old Yupoo albums, a planner pays for itself in fewer wrong sizes and lost links.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
