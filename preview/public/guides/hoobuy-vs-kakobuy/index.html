Hoobuy vs Kakobuy: reliability vs polish

Both are newer, cheaper agents fighting for the same buyer. One has the community's trust. The other has the polish. Ratings and features checked July 2026 — recheck before you commit a parcel.

The short answer

Hoobuy on trust. Kakobuy on polish. Keep a backup either way.

Community ratings in mid-2026 put Hoobuy near 4.1 out of 5 across hundreds of reviews — fast buying, reliable QC, few support horror stories. Kakobuy sits near 2.7 out of 5 on a smaller count, with slow refunds the recurring complaint.

Kakobuy is the nicer app: modern interface, five to eight free QC photos an item, QC back in 6–12 hours, and strong tax-free lines into Europe. Hoobuy is the steadier operator: QC in 12–24 hours, plainer site, stronger lines to the US and Asia-Pacific.

Read ratings right

The count matters as much as the score

A 2.7 from fifty reviews and a 4.1 from four hundred are not the same kind of fact. The large sample is the reliable one. Small samples swing on one bad week.

The pattern that holds across every agent: support quality is invisible until something goes wrong. A refund that takes a month is the real fee. Budget agents are cheap precisely because some of the risk moves back to you.

Where each wins

Different strengths for different parcels

  • Hoobuy wins reliability. Higher rating, larger sample, steady QC, fewer refund complaints.
  • Kakobuy wins the photo count. Five to eight free QC photos an item, back fast.
  • Kakobuy wins Europe. Its tax-free (triangle) lines are the draw for EU buyers.
  • Hoobuy wins the US. Stronger US and Asia-Pacific line coverage.
The backup rule

Every experienced buyer keeps two agents

Newer agents change fast — in both directions. The community's standard advice is a backup agent with a small balance, so a frozen parcel or a slow refund never holds your whole haul hostage.

This is cheap to do when your list is portable. Credenza stores the canonical store link, never the agent wrap, so your whole shelf works at the backup agent the day you need it.
