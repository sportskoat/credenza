CNFans vs Superbuy: the beginner's agent vs the budget pick

CNFans costs less on most hauls. Superbuy moves faster and answers faster. The right pick depends on what your mistake would cost you. Numbers checked July 2026 — rates change, so recheck before a big parcel.

The short answer

First haul: Superbuy. Tenth haul: run the CNFans math.

Superbuy has run since 2012. It buys fast (1–3 days to the warehouse), its support answers, and its site explains itself. You pay for that: a service fee around 5% on typical orders, and only three free QC photos an item — extra angles cost extra.

CNFans advertises a 0% service fee and gives QC photos away free and unlimited. It makes its money in two quieter places: the exchange rate and the shipping bill. Community comparisons put its hidden exchange markup near 6–7% in early 2026.

The trap

"0% fee" does not mean cheaper. Add the whole bill.

Compare total landed cost, not the headline fee: item price, plus service fee, plus the exchange-rate gap, plus shipping. A 0% fee with a bad rate can cost more than a 5% fee with a fair one.

On a benchmark 5 kg parcel to the US in early 2026, community comparisons landed within five dollars of each other: CNFans near $84 all-in, Superbuy near $88. Close enough that speed and support decide it for most people.

Where each wins

Speed and photos pull opposite ways

  • Superbuy wins speed. 1–3 day buying, a mature support desk, and a direct escalation path. After a late-2025 warehouse move, CNFans buyers reported 5–12 day processing on some orders.
  • CNFans wins QC photos. Free and unlimited. Superbuy gives three an item and charges for more.
  • Superbuy wins the first haul. Fewer ways to make an expensive mistake.
  • CNFans wins the big haul. Free photos across thirty items is real money, and experienced buyers route around the slower weeks.
Where a planner helps

You do not have to marry one agent

  • Credenza stores the canonical store link, never the agent wrap. Switching agent is one setting, not a lost list.
  • The weight estimate prices your parcel before you commit, so the shipping half of the math is visible up front.
  • QC photos attach to the card whichever agent took them, beside the item details.
Honest disclosure

What Credenza earns here

Buy links may include a referral code. Credenza may earn a commission on agent shipping fees. A referral never changes your item price. CNFans stopped its referral programme, so Credenza earns nothing when you pick it — it is on this page because the math says it belongs.

Rates above come from community comparisons, July 2026. Agents change fees without notice. Recheck both sites before a big parcel.
