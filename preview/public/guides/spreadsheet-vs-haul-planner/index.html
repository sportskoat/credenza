Spreadsheet vs haul planner

Both can list links. Only one keeps photo, size decision, QC, and agent handoff on the same card.

Spreadsheet wins when

Simple lists and one buyer habit

  • You store only URLs and a price column.
  • You never attach QC photos to rows.
  • Size is “guess L” with no chart on the row.
  • One person, few items, rare reorders.
Planner wins when

Decisions need context

  • Yupoo photos and Weidian buy links must stay paired.
  • You match a seller chart to a body profile (in or cm).
  • QC photos must stay beside the item before you ship the parcel.
  • You open Superbuy or another agent without rewriting every stored URL.
Credenza

What the planner is (and is not)

Credenza is an agent haul planner: stash finds, size, mark purchases, open Buy in your agent. It is not a marketplace, not a search engine for sellers, and not a checkout.

Money model: free planner; optional agent affiliate only when you open Buy on a participating agent.

The real difference

A row holds text. A decision needs a picture.

A sheet is excellent at what a sheet is for: numbers in columns, sorted and summed. Agent shopping asks three questions a column cannot answer.

  • Is this the item in the photo? The Yupoo album and the Weidian link are two different URLs for one thing. In a sheet they are two cells, and nothing keeps them together.
  • What size? The chart is a screenshot. Your measurements are somewhere else. The comparison happens in your head, once for each item, every time.
  • Did the QC pass? Four photos an item, arriving days apart. A sheet cell holds one line of text.
Where a sheet actually breaks

The agent column

The common sheet layout stores the agent link, because that is the link you clicked. Then you switch agents, and every row is wrong.

Find and replace does not fix it. Some agents want the whole URL, and others want only the numeric item id — and the id is inside the original link the agent wrap replaced. The three link shapes explains why. Store the marketplace link and wrap it at Buy, and switching is one setting.

Shared sheets

A link to the sheet shows the whole sheet

Asking a group about two items means sharing every item, every price, and every note you wrote about the seller. Most people accept that or paste screenshots instead.

A read-only share link carries the items you pick and the fields you pick, so prices can stay off. Delete it when the thread ends.

Migration

Leave the sheet without losing history

Paste links into Credenza as you decide. Keep the old sheet as archive if you want. You do not need a full import day-one—start with the active haul.

A haul post pastes in one go and comes out as one card an item. See turning a Reddit haul into a list.

Leaving again

The export goes back to a sheet

A planner you cannot leave is a worse deal than a sheet. Two ways out exist.

  • Backup file (.json) — free, on every plan. It holds the whole shelf and restores it.
  • Spreadsheet export (.csv) — a Pro feature. It opens in the spreadsheet you came from, with the columns you would have kept by hand: title, haul, status, seller, size, recommended size, price, currency, price in USD, weight, buy link, photos link, source link, notes, and the date added.

Everything is stored on your device first, so the data is yours whether or not you keep using the app.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
