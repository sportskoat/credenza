Guides for agent haul planning

Clear answers to buying questions: organize links, size with confidence, open your agent. Not a product catalog.

Every guide below answers one question end to end. They are ordered by where a haul actually goes: you collect links, you decide a size, you judge the QC photos, and you open Buy in an agent.

2 · Decide the size

This is the expensive stage. A size chart on a listing is measured flat, in centimetres, garment by garment — so the same label is a different garment across two stores. A wrong size is not a return; it is a second parcel and a second shipping fee. Do this before you buy, not after.

What these guides assume

You already found the items. You already have an agent account, or you are choosing one. Nothing here tells you what to buy, and no guide names a store or a seller — Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace. The links are yours; the guides are about what to do with them.

Every guide maps to something the app does. Credenza reads a link into a card, keeps your measurements next to the chart, holds QC photos on the item, and marks each one as Want or Bought. The guide explains the decision; the app remembers it.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
