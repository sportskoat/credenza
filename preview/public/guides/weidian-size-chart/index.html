Why charts fail in chat

Screenshots are not decisions

Sellers post cm tables, sometimes hip-only. Agents do not remember your body. You need the chart next to the item and a stored profile you trust.

In Credenza

Profile + chart on the card

  • Set body profile once (in or cm; storage stays metric under the hood).
  • Paste or attach size chart text for the item (Yupoo album notes when you use that path).
  • Read “your size” with a reason line—not a silent magic number.
  • Shoes, hats, and bags skip clothing-style recommendation when it does not apply.
Tradeoff

Still not a tailor

Recommendations use the chart and ease rules you can inspect. Final fit risk stays with the seller and the agent purchase—Credenza reduces avoidable mistakes; it does not guarantee fit.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
