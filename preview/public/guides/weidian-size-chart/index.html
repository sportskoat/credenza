Why charts fail in chat

Screenshots are not decisions

Sellers post cm tables, sometimes hip-only. Agents do not remember your body. You need the chart next to the item and a stored profile you trust.

In Credenza

Profile + chart on the card

  • Set body profile once (in or cm; storage stays metric under the hood).
  • Paste or attach size chart text for the item (Yupoo album notes when you use that path).
  • Read “your size” with a reason line—not a silent magic number.
  • Shoes, hats, and bags skip clothing-style recommendation when it does not apply.
The two layouts

Labeled lines, or a table

Almost every seller chart is one of two shapes, and Credenza reads both.

  • Labeled — one line for each size, with the measure beside the number: M: 胸围112 衣长70 肩宽48.
  • Table — a header row of measures, then one row for each size.

The labels can be Chinese or English, and the two are often mixed in the same chart. 胸围 and chest are the same column. So are 腰围 and waist, 臀围 and hip, 肩宽 and shoulder, 袖长 and sleeve, 衣长 and length.

The trap

Half chest reads as a size too small

Chinese tee charts often print 半胸 — half chest, measured flat across the garment, also written pit to pit. The number is half the circumference. A 56 cm half chest is a 112 cm garment.

Read it as a full measure and every size looks four sizes too small, so you size up twice and receive a tent. Credenza doubles the column when the numbers sit in the half-chest band, or when the text says flat or half.

The math

Ease is why the chart number is bigger than you

A chart lists the garment, not the body. The gap between the two is ease, and the right amount depends on what the item is.

  • Tops — about 12 cm over your chest.
  • Outerwear — about 16 cm, because something goes under it.
  • Pants and shorts — about 2 cm over your waist. A waistband does not drape.

The reason line names the measure it used and the gap it found, so you can disagree with it.

Hip-only charts

Yupoo pants sheets often list one column

Plenty of pants charts print 臀围 and nothing else. A tool that requires a waist column returns nothing at all, which is the least useful answer.

Credenza falls back to hip, and says that is what it used. Store both numbers once in your measurements and either chart shape works.

When the seller warns you

Runs big and runs small are read, not ignored

Chart text that says 偏大, 偏小, 正码, runs big, runs small or true to size is picked up and shown with the pick. It does not silently move the size. You see the note and decide.

Tradeoff

Still not a tailor

Recommendations use the chart and ease rules you can inspect. Final fit risk stays with the seller and the agent purchase—Credenza reduces avoidable mistakes; it does not guarantee fit.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
