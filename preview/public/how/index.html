How Credenza works

One shelf for the whole haul: stash finds, decide size, keep QC context, open Buy in your preferred shopping agent.

Open app FAQ llms.txt
Step 1

Stash the find

Paste a Weidian, Taobao, or Yupoo link, or a line with photos + buy URLs. Credenza keeps them on one card so you are not juggling tabs and a spreadsheet.

Step 2

See what you saved

Cover image, title, price, and seller in a quiet card. Flip for notes, status, and more photos. The grid and the open card use the same mental model.

Step 3

Size with confidence

Save a body profile (inches or cm). When a size chart is available (including hip-only 臀围 sheets), Credenza recommends a size and shows why.

Step 4

Run the haul pipeline

Want → Bought → QC → GL / RL → Shipped. Status lives on the item so the active haul stays legible when the warehouse photos land.

Step 5

Open Buy in your agent

Items store the canonical marketplace URL. At Buy time, Credenza opens Superbuy, Sugargoo, Kakobuy, or your preferred agent. Purchases happen there, not inside Credenza.

Credenza is a decision layer, not a marketplace and not a W2C search engine. You bring the links from communities and stores you already use.
