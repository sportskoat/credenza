Why there is a limit at all

The AI calls cost money

Reading a size chart and resolving a Weidian link each call a paid model. Free includes fixed allowances. Pro adds monthly allowances. Everything else on the shelf runs on your device and is free. Read what a link resolve is for what one actually buys you.

Your shelf

Your cards stay yours

The shelf lives on your device. If Pro ends, nothing is deleted: every card, every haul, and every QC photo you already saved stays where it is. Only new additions go back to the free caps. The haul row counts open hauls, not hauls you have ever run, so archiving a shipped one frees a slot on any plan: closing a haul covers what archiving keeps and what it hides. For the same promise checked one row at a time, read what happens when Pro ends.

Devices and links

Shared links and device sync

A shared link is a read-only page of one haul. Free keeps 3 links at a time. Pro keeps 100, and adds unlisted links, an expiry date, and a link with no Credenza footer. Delete a link at any time from Profile → Shared links.

Unlisted is narrower than it sounds, so it is worth reading before you buy for it: what an unlisted link hides is the preview card a chat draws when the address is pasted. The page itself opens for anyone holding the address either way.

Devices work the same way for everybody in one direction. Sign in on a new phone and your shelf comes back: that is free, because losing a phone must not cost you your cards. Pro is the other direction: every edit keeps going up, so two devices stay in step while you use them.

Billing

Cancel whenever you want

Stripe handles the payment and the receipts. Open the customer portal from your profile in the app to change the plan or cancel. Credenza never sees your card number.

When the free plan is enough

Most people do not need Pro. The shelf, the parcel planner, the Reddit paste, and Buy in your agent remain free. Pro adds monthly reads after you use the Free allowance.

Stay on free if this is you

You run one or two hauls at a time. You add finds over weeks, not in one sitting. You read size charts yourself, or you buy the same size from a store you already know. Eight AI chart reads and eight link resolves let you test the complete workflow before paying. A saved chart can be reused on the same item without spending another read. what spends a chart read lists everything that costs nothing.

What the free plan covers lists every number, and the parts that are not counted at all. Cards on your shelf are the largest of those: how many a shelf holds says why that row reads Unlimited on both plans, and what can still run out on a phone full of QC photos.

Pro pays for itself if this is you

You paste a whole Reddit haul at once, so eight resolves runs out in one sitting. You keep several hauls open — a summer order, a winter order, a group buy. You size every item from its chart rather than guessing, which is the habit that costs the most reads and saves the most reshipping.

One wrong size costs a second parcel and a second shipping fee. That is usually more than a year of Pro. We would still rather you started free and found out.

Paying, and getting your money back

The weekly plan starts with 3 days free. Stripe collects your card up front and the first $2.49 charge lands on day 4. Cancel from the customer portal before day 4 and you pay nothing. Monthly and yearly carry no trial: the free plan needs no card and does not expire, so you can test everything that matters before you decide.

If you do pay and change your mind, write to support@credenzafashion.com within 14 days of the charge. We refund it in full, for any reason, and we do not ask you why. After 14 days we refund the unused part of an annual plan if Credenza stops working for reasons on our side. The Terms say the same thing in full.

Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not refund the period you are in on its own: ask, within the 14 days, and it does.

Common questions

Is Credenza Fashion free?

Yes. The shelf holds unlimited saved cards. A free account includes eight card reads and eight chart reads. Pro adds monthly usage.

What does Credenza Fashion Pro cost?

Pro is $2.49 a week, $5.99 a month, or $44.99 a year. The weekly plan starts with 3 days free: your card is collected up front, the first charge lands on day 4, and you can cancel before then and pay nothing. The yearly plan saves 37% and works out at $3.75 a month.

Does Credenza buy the item for me?

No. Credenza is the decision layer before the agent click. You pay your own shopping agent: Superbuy, Sugargoo, Kakobuy, or another. Credenza never handles the purchase.

Can I cancel Credenza Fashion Pro?

Yes. Cancel at any time from the Stripe customer portal in the app. Cancel during the free trial and you pay nothing. Your shelf and every card stay on your device after Pro ends.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Write to support@credenzafashion.com within 14 days of a charge and we refund it in full, for any reason. After 14 days we refund the unused part of an annual plan if Credenza stops working for reasons on our side.

Do I need Pro to use Credenza Fashion?

No. The shelf, parcel planner, Reddit haul paste, and Buy remain free. Pro adds monthly reads after the Free allowance.

Credenza organizes your haul. Purchases and payments still happen with your shopping agent.
