Organize

One shelf, not a spreadsheet

Paste a link. Credenza builds a card with the photos, the price, and the seller. Group the cards into hauls with a budget and a parcel estimate.

Size

A size pick from your measurements

Save your body profile once. Credenza reads the seller size chart and recommends a size. No chart? Your usual sizes show as an estimate. The math runs on your device.

QC

A QC review checklist

Attach the warehouse photos. Mark GL or RL. Track the item from want to shipped, with the weight and the chargeable parcel estimate in view.

Buy

Your agent, one tap

Buy opens the item in Superbuy, CNFans, Mulebuy, Kakobuy, or your preferred agent. Credenza never checks out for you. Some agent links carry a referral code that supports the app at no cost to you.

Credenza is an international agent wishlist and planner. It is not a marketplace and not a search engine. You bring the links from the stores and the communities you already use. Your data stays on your device.
