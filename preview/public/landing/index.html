Sizing

A size pick that shows its work.

Save your measurements once. Credenza reads the seller's chart, from the listing text or the photos of it, then lays their numbers against yours — one row per measurement, ease in centimetres. No chart on the listing? It says so and falls back to your usual size rather than inventing a confident answer.

What Credenza isn't

A planner, not a store.

Not a marketplace

Nothing is for sale here. Credenza holds links to listings you found yourself and hands them to your agent.

Not a search engine

It won't find items for you. You bring the links from the stores and communities you already use.

Not a checkout

No card details, no payments. Buy opens your agent's page and the transaction happens there.
