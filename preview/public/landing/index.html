Sizing

A size pick that shows its work.

Save your measurements once. Credenza reads the seller's chart, from the listing text or the photos of it, then compares it to you and tells you which size and why in one sentence. No chart on the listing? It says so and falls back to your usual size rather than inventing a confident answer.

Reads the chart out of the listing text, or out of the album photos when the seller only posts an image.
Tells you the ease in centimetres, not a vague “runs small”.

What it costs

Free is the whole app. Pro is more of it.

Every feature on this page works on the free plan. Nothing is held back behind a wall. What Pro raises is how much of it you can do in a day, and how many hauls you can keep open at once.

Free · $0

Unlimited cards. 2 hauls at once, 4 QC photos an item. 3 shared links. Link resolves run 20 a day, size-chart reads 2 a day. No card, no trial clock, no expiry.

Pro · $2.49 a week

100 hauls, 12 QC photos an item. 100 shared links. Link resolves run 250 a day, size-chart reads 15 a day. Your shelf stays in step across every device. 3 days free on the weekly plan. $44.99 a year is $3.75 a month.

If Pro ends

Nothing you saved is deleted. Every card, haul, QC photo and shared link stays. Only new additions return to the free caps — checked row by row.

The full table is on the pricing page. Start free and stay free if the caps suit you — most people never reach them.

What Credenza isn't

A planner, not a store.

Not a marketplace

Nothing is for sale here. Credenza holds links to listings you found yourself and hands them to your agent.

Not a search engine

It won't find items for you. You bring the links from the stores and communities you already use.

Not a checkout

No card details, no payments. Buy opens your agent's page and the transaction happens there.
